Please choose the nearest square rather than giving your house number. A report stores the grid square, not your exact address.

To help identify duplicate or abusive reports and diagnose technical problems, the private report log also stores basic browser, device and connection information supplied by your browser. It does not create a persistent device ID, and this setup cannot see or record your IP address.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Square colours represent aggregated reports and should be interpreted alongside the number of reports in each square. The public map contains aggregate grid statistics only; comments and technical report metadata remain in the private reporting sheet.
